A Material for Every Project

Every material reacts differently to laser engraving. Tell us what you are working with and what you want to create. We will help determine whether the project is a good fit for our equipment.

Material 01

Wood

Wood is one of the most versatile materials for laser engraving. The laser creates a permanent design directly in the surface, giving each piece its own character.

Popular Wood Projects

  • Personalized signs
  • Plaques and awards
  • Cutting boards
  • Keepsake boxes
  • Retirement gifts
  • Home decor
  • Custom gifts
Ask About Wood Engraving

Material 02

Glass

Laser engraving creates a permanent frosted appearance on glass. It works well for personalized gifts, recognition pieces and keepsakes.

Popular Glass Projects

  • Drinking glasses
  • Mugs
  • Awards
  • Personalized gifts
  • Wedding pieces
  • Memorial items
  • Custom keepsakes
Ask About Glass Engraving

Material 03

Ceramics

Certain ceramic products can be customized with laser engraving to create a permanent design, message, logo or other personalized detail.

Ceramic Project Ideas

  • Mugs
  • Tiles
  • Decorative pieces
  • Personalized gifts
  • Memorial pieces
  • Custom designs

Because ceramic finishes vary, we recommend sending us a photo of the item before bringing it in.

Ask About Ceramic Engraving

Material 04

Leather

Laser engraving can add names, artwork, logos and personalized designs directly to many leather products.

Popular Leather Projects

  • Wallets
  • Journals
  • Patches
  • Keychains
  • Tags
  • Personalized gifts

Different types of natural and synthetic leather can react differently to laser engraving, so we will review your item before starting the project.

Ask About Leather Engraving
