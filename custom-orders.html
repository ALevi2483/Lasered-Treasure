Start Your Project

How It Works

From Idea to Finished Project

Custom engraving does not need to be complicated. Here is what to expect when starting a project with us.

1

Tell Us Your Idea

Describe what you want made, personalized or engraved. Include the type of item, quantity and any important details.

2

Send Photos or Artwork

Upload a photo of the item, your logo, artwork, inspiration photo or anything else that helps explain the project.

3

We Review the Project

We will review the material, design, size, quantity and other details to determine what is possible.

4

Receive Your Quote

Once we understand the project, we will provide pricing and discuss any remaining details before work begins.

5

Approve the Details

Confirm the design, wording, quantity and project details before your item moves into production.

6

We Create It

Your project is engraved and prepared for pickup, delivery or shipping based on the arrangements made for your order.

Already Have the Item?

Customer Supplied Items

Have something you already own that you would like engraved? Send us photos before bringing it in.

We need to review the item's material, shape, size and surface before determining whether it can be safely engraved.

  • Take clear photos from multiple angles.
  • Tell us what the item is made from, if known.
  • Include approximate dimensions.
  • Show us where you want the engraving placed.
  • Send the wording, logo or design you want engraved.

Before You Submit

A Few Things to Know

Custom Means Custom

Pricing can vary based on the item, material, artwork, engraving area, quantity and project complexity.

Give Us Time

If your project is for a retirement, wedding, birthday or other event, contact us as early as possible.

Double Check Names

Review names, dates, ranks, titles and other personalized text carefully before approving your project.

Ask Questions

If you are not sure whether your idea will work, ask. A photo is usually the easiest place to start.
