Our Story

About Lasered Treasure

Custom engraving is about more than putting a name on an object. It is about creating something personal that was made for a specific person, event or moment.

Portsmouth, Virginia

Creating Something Worth Keeping

Lasered Treasure is a woman-owned engraving business located in Portsmouth, Virginia and led by Carol Davis.

We create custom engraved pieces for customers looking for something personal, useful or meaningful. Projects range from gifts and keepsakes to awards, plaques, signs and items customers bring to us with their own ideas.

Because custom projects do not always fit neatly into a catalog, we encourage customers to reach out with photos, artwork or even a rough idea. We can review the project and help determine what may be possible.

Carol Davis Lasered Treasure

How We Work

The Details Matter

Every custom project has its own purpose, material, design and story. We treat each one accordingly.

01

Personal

Your project should feel like yours. Names, dates, artwork and details help turn an ordinary item into something created for a specific person.

02

Custom

Not every project comes from a catalog. We welcome ideas, inspiration photos, unusual items and requests that need a little discussion first.

03

Clear

We want to understand what you are asking for before work starts. That means reviewing the material, artwork, wording, quantity and timing.

Local
